About Shot By Diallo

Shot By Diallo produces business films, recurring video content and selected artist visuals in Greater Montréal. The work starts with the audience and the question the film needs to answer.

Discuss your shoot

What goes into the brief

Before choosing locations or shots, identify the people who will watch, what they need to understand and where the video will appear. That decision guides the interview questions, the framing and the versions to deliver.

A production you can follow

Agree on the scope, key dates and the person who will review the edit. Use the shoot plan to coordinate people and locations, then review the cut against the original brief. For recurring work, use the next planning conversation to set priorities for the following shoot.

See the work and get in touch

Browse the portfolio for retail, sport and storytelling projects. To discuss a shoot in Montréal, Longueuil, Brossard, Laval or elsewhere on the South Shore, include the location and intended use in your project request.
