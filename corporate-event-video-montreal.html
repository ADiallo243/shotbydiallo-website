Corporate event video production in Montreal

Decide what the event should leave behind. A short recap, a speaker excerpt and a record of a presentation require different coverage.

Mark the moments that happen once

Walk through the run of show with the organizer. Identify awards, entrances and announcements that cannot be repeated. When activities overlap, prioritize coverage before confirming the production scope.

Coordinate sound and access

Check microphone feeds with the venue and agree on camera positions, load-in and attendee permissions. Reserve a quieter area if the edit calls for participant interviews.

Plan delivery around the follow-up

A recap for attendees can emphasize shared moments. A clip for next year’s invitation needs to explain the experience to someone new. Agree on the first delivery date, captions and priority excerpts before the event.
