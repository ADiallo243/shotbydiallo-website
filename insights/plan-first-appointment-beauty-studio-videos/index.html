Three videos to prepare a first-time beauty studio visitor

Use questions people ask before booking to plan three short answers about the location, the practitioner and the appointment.

Help visitors find the studio

Film the entrance as a visitor sees it from the street, followed by the reception point. Check that signs remain legible on a phone. Keep booking screens, names and reflections of people waiting outside the frame.

Introduce the person receiving the client

Ask the practitioner to introduce themselves and explain what they discuss at the start of an appointment. Record in a quiet space. Have them check the spelling of their name, their role and the captions.

Explain one appointment step

Choose a step that staff can stage. Agree with the participant on the framing and intended use. End with a way to ask the studio a question. Keep private client records and claims about results out of the edit.
