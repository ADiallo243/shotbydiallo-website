How to film a first class at a fitness studio

A prospective member often wants to know what happens when they arrive. Build a short walkthrough around the questions your front desk actually hears.

Map a first visit

Walk the route from the entrance to the end of the class. Where does a newcomer check in, leave a bag and meet the coach? Ask reception which two steps cause the most confusion. Give each a clear shot.

Schedule around the session

Film the entrance and equipment before the group arrives. Agree with the coach where the camera can stand without blocking participants. Reserve a quiet few minutes after the session to record instructions.

Review with a new viewer

Ask someone unfamiliar with the studio to watch the cut and tell you where to go, what to bring and whom to ask for help. If they miss a step, you know exactly which shot or caption needs another pass.
