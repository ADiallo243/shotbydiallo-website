Monthly Video Production in Montreal

A filming plan for the month ahead

When your business has a regular stream of launches, classes or customer questions, planning several videos together gives each shoot a clear purpose.

Discuss a monthly production plan

Choose what belongs in this cycle

Bring your promotion dates, recurring customer questions and the channels you already use. For a fitness studio, for example, a class introduction and a coach interview may share a location but need different shots and approvals.

Build the shoot around the people involved

Group scenes by location and availability. Decide which interviews need quiet, who can approve talking points and which scenes can be filmed while the business stays open.

Agree on the edits before filming

A website introduction, a vertical cut and a captioned interview excerpt each need different framing. Agree on formats, review dates and file delivery before reserving the shoot. The number of edits and the cadence depend on the brief.

Use the monthly content worksheet to group filming by location and subject.
