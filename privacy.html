Privacy

Your information,
handled with care.

This policy explains what this website collects, why it is collected and the choices available to you.

Effective date: August 12, 2026

Who is responsible

Shot By Diallo is responsible for personal information collected through this website. Questions, access requests or privacy concerns can be sent to shotbydiallo@gmail.com.

Information we collect

When you submit a project request, we collect the information you choose to provide, including your name, contact details, company or artist name, service, budget range, timeline, location, project description, referral information and preferred contact method.

Our hosting and security providers may also process basic technical information such as an IP address, browser type, request time and requested page to deliver and protect the site.

How we use it

  • Review and reply to project requests.
  • Plan, quote and deliver requested services.
  • Maintain customer and project records.
  • Protect the website and prevent spam or abuse.
  • Measure and improve the website when analytics consent is provided.

Service providers and transfers

The website uses Vercel for hosting, Supabase for secure project-request storage, Resend for lead-notification email and Google Analytics for optional audience measurement. These providers process information on our behalf and may process it outside Quebec or Canada, subject to their safeguards and terms.

Google Analytics and cookies

Google Analytics is optional and remains disabled until you select “Accept analytics.” If accepted, it helps us understand visits, interactions and successful project requests. You can reject optional analytics without affecting the website or contact form.

Your choice is saved in your browser. Select the button below at any time to review it. For more detail about Google’s handling of Analytics information, see Google’s privacy policy.

Retention and protection

We retain project and customer information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, business and tax records, dispute resolution and legal obligations. We use access controls and reputable service providers to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, loss or misuse.

Your choices and rights

You may ask to access or correct personal information we hold about you, withdraw consent where applicable, or ask a question about its use. Contact shotbydiallo@gmail.com. We may need to confirm your identity before completing a request.

Updates

This policy may be updated when our services, tools or legal obligations change. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are published.
