Who is responsible

Shot By Diallo is responsible for personal information collected through this website. Questions, access requests or privacy concerns can be sent to shotbydiallo@gmail.com.

Information we collect

A project request may include your name, contact details, company or artist name, service, budget, timeline, location and project description.

Our hosting and security providers may also process basic technical information such as an IP address, browser type, request time and requested page to deliver and protect the site.

How we use it

  • Review and reply to project requests.
  • Plan, quote and deliver requested services.
  • Maintain customer and project records.
  • Protect the website and prevent spam or abuse.
  • Measure and improve the website when analytics consent is provided.

Service providers and transfers

Vercel hosts the site, Supabase stores project requests, Resend sends notifications and Google Analytics provides optional audience measurement. Information may be processed outside Quebec or Canada under their safeguards and terms.

Cookies and Google Analytics

Google Analytics remains off until you select “Accept all.” If accepted, it measures visits and interactions so we can improve the site. Refusing does not affect the website or contact form.

Your choice is saved in your browser. Select the button below at any time to review it. For more detail about Google’s handling of Analytics information, see Google’s privacy policy.

Retention and protection

We retain information only as long as needed for projects, business records and legal obligations. Access controls and reputable providers help reduce unauthorized access, loss or misuse.

Your choices and rights

You may request access or correction, withdraw consent where applicable, or ask how information is used. Email shotbydiallo@gmail.com. We may need to confirm your identity.

Updates

This policy may be updated when our services, tools or legal obligations change. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are published.
