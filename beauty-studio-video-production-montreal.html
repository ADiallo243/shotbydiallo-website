Video production for beauty and aesthetics businesses in Montreal

Answer the questions a first-time visitor has before booking. A studio introduction and a practitioner-led explanation give future clients a clear idea of the appointment.

Discuss your shoot

Start with the booking questions

Ask reception which questions come up most often. Pick one per clip, such as where to arrive or what the initial conversation covers. Have the practitioner review the wording before filming.

Prepare a controlled demonstration

Agree on which steps can be shown and who has agreed to appear. Arrange a staged appointment if a real appointment would interfere with privacy. Check mirrors, screens and background audio before recording.

Create material staff can reuse

Pair a studio introduction for the booking page with short answers staff can share when a client asks a familiar question. Review service descriptions and captions before publishing each version.
